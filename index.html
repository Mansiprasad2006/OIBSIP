Considered by design

Fabric

Mid-weight wool, brushed cotton twill, and undyed linen sourced from mills that have made the same cloth for three generations. Nothing that pills, nothing that needs a warning label.

Fit

Every silhouette is graded from a block built for movement first — sitting, reaching, walking fast to catch a train — then adjusted until it also happens to look sharp standing still.

Finish

Flat-felled seams, horn buttons, no visible logos. The kind of detailing you notice on the inside of a garment, six months in, when it still hangs the way it did on day one.

“We stopped asking what would sell this season, and started asking what we'd still be wearing in five years.”

Nadia Orelle, founder

Made to be worn out, not thrown out

Orelle started in 2019 as a single coat, cut from a bolt of leftover wool a tailor in Porto couldn't sell. We still work with small mills and family-run cutting rooms today, releasing four collections a year instead of fifty-two, and repairing anything we've made, free, for as long as you own it.

No fast turnarounds, no disposable basics — just clothing built to outlast the trend it was bought for.
