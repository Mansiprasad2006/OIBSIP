Mira Voss

Private chef & seasonal table

Cooking for small tables around a single idea: what's good today, treated simply and served warm.

MV

Mira trained in small kitchens before spending six years cooking on farms, where the harvest set the menu, not the calendar. She now runs a private dining table for groups of two to twelve, built entirely around what's ready to pick, catch, or ferment that week. Every booking starts with a conversation, not a fixed menu.
